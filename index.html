Product Overview

The 80GHz Radar Water Level Sensor is designed for accurate and reliable water level measurement in rivers, reservoirs, canals, flood monitoring stations and smart water management systems.

Using advanced 80GHz radar technology, the sensor provides non-contact measurement with excellent stability under rain, fog, wind and harsh outdoor environments.

Key Features

80GHz Radar Technology

High focusing beam and strong anti-interference capability.

Non-Contact Measurement

No sensor contact with water surface.

High Accuracy

Suitable for professional hydrological monitoring.

All Weather Operation

Reliable performance under rain, fog and snow.

IP68 Protection

Designed for outdoor long-term installation.

RS485 Modbus

Easy integration with SCADA and IoT systems.
